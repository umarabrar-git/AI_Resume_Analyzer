Privacy & Data

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Resume Analyzer handles information when you use our resume analysis, resume creation, job matching, reporting, and AI-assisted career tools.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, the application may process information such as account details, uploaded resumes, job descriptions, professional skills, and information you provide while using career-related tools.

2. How We Use Information

  • Analyze resume content and structure.
  • Calculate ATS and job-match insights.
  • Identify skills and potential skill gaps.
  • Generate resume and career recommendations.
  • Provide application features and improve user experience.

3. Resume and Career Data

Resume files can contain personal and professional information. The application processes this information only as needed to provide the features requested by the user.

4. Data Security

We aim to use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no digital system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Third-Party Services

Some application functionality may rely on third-party technologies or services. Their use may be subject to their own privacy practices and terms.

6. User Choices

Users should only upload information they are authorized to use. Where account-management functionality is available, users may manage their account information through the application.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application features or data practices change. The latest revision date will be displayed on this page.

8. Contact

Questions regarding privacy or data handling can be directed through the application's official support or contact channel when available.
