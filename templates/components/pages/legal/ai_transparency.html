Responsible AI

AI & Data Transparency

Effective July 26, 2026

AI Resume Analyzer uses automated techniques to assist with resume analysis, content generation and career-related insights.

How AI Is Used

  • Resume content analysis.
  • Skill identification.
  • Job-description matching.
  • Resume-writing assistance.
  • Career-related recommendations.
AI can make mistakes

Generated recommendations and scores should be reviewed by the user before making important career decisions.

Human Decision Making

The platform is intended to assist users rather than replace human judgment in employment or career decisions.
