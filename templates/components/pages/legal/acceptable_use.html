Platform Policy

Acceptable Use Policy

Effective July 26, 2026

AI Resume Analyzer should be used responsibly and in ways that respect other users, platform security and applicable law.

Permitted Use

  • Resume analysis and improvement.
  • Job-description matching.
  • Professional resume creation.
  • Career development and skill analysis.

Prohibited Use

  • Unauthorized access to accounts or systems.
  • Uploading malicious files or code.
  • Attempting to bypass platform usage limits.
  • Reverse engineering protected platform systems.
  • Using the platform for unlawful discrimination.
  • Submitting information you are not authorized to use.
