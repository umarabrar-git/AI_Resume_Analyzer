Privacy Policy

Effective July 26, 2026

Your resume can contain important personal and professional information. This policy explains what information the platform processes and why.

1. Information We Process

  • Account information: name, email and account-related information.
  • Resume information: work history, education, skills, certifications and other information contained in uploaded documents.
  • Job information: job descriptions and matching criteria submitted for analysis.
  • Usage information: technical and product usage information required to operate and improve the service.

2. How Information Is Used

  • Parse and analyze resumes.
  • Calculate ATS-related insights.
  • Compare resumes with job descriptions.
  • Identify skills and potential skill gaps.
  • Generate requested AI-assisted recommendations.
  • Maintain and improve platform reliability.
Your career data matters

Resume and career information should only be used according to the choices and services presented to the user.

3. Data Retention

Retention periods should depend on account status, service requirements and applicable legal obligations. Where deletion controls are available, users can request removal of eligible account information.

4. Data Security

We aim to use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect information processed through the platform.
